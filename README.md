Problem Statement

Understanding who a customer base actually is, and which segments purchase the most, is critical for targeted marketing and resource allocation. This project analyzes KPMG's 2010 customer data to answer

which job industries generate the highest average purchase value How customer purchasing relates to tenure Which states and age groups the customer base is concentrated in How purchasing differs by gender and wealth segment

Dataset

Attribute: 
Description Period: Year 2010
Fields: Job Industry, State, Gender, Age, Tenure, Wealth Segment, Purchase Value


Methodology

Data Modeling, connecting job industry, state, gender, age, and wealth segment dimensions

KPI Design, built core measures for average purchase by job industry, purchase by wealth segment, and customer counts using DAX

Dashboard Design, built an interactive dashboard with slicers for job industry and state

Trend Analysis, visualized purchase versus tenure, age distribution, and customer counts to surface demographic and behavioral patterns

Key Findings

Job Industry Performance:  Agriculture is the best purchased job industry at 106.12 percent average purchase, ahead of Health (101.04 percent) and Property (100.34 percent), while Retail is the lowest performing job category overall.

Purchase vs Tenure : Purchase value declines as tenure decreases across the plotted points, moving from 640 down to 565, suggesting a relationship between how long a customer has been with the business and their purchase value that is worth investigating further.

Geographic Concentration:  New South Wales is by far the largest customer base at 2089 customers, followed by Victoria (998) and Queensland (821). Victoria is also flagged as the second best performing state.

Gender Distribution : Female customers slightly outnumber male customers, 2037 versus 1871, and female is identified as the most outstanding gender by purchasing behavior.

Wealth Segment Mass:  Customers make up the largest purchasing segment at 97202, well ahead of High Net Worth (47270) and Affluent Customers (47138), showing the business is heavily reliant on its mass market segment.

Job Industry by Volume : Manufacturing (796) and Financial Services (766) are the largest customer segments by job industry, followed by Health (596) and Retail (358), showing some difference between which industries purchase the most per customer versus which have the most customers overall.


Conclusion

KPMG's 2010 customer base is concentrated in New South Wales, skews slightly female, and is dominated by the Mass Customer wealth segment, while Agriculture customers deliver the highest average purchase value despite Manufacturing and Financial Services having the largest customer counts. These findings can guide targeted marketing toward high value segments and industries.
